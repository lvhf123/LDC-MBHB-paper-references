# Reading list: SBI for LISA MBHBs (LDC1-1 Radler paper)

Prepared for writing our paper *Dynamic SBI for MBHB parameter estimation with LISA* (Radler LDC1-1 MBHB, Falcon, truncated sequential flow-matching posterior estimation).

The list comes from the INSPIRE-HEP citation graph around 20 seed papers (all papers citing them and all papers they cite), plus INSPIRE full-text searches for "Radler", "LDC-1A", "Sangria" and "Spritz" and keyword searches on arXiv. Each entry was checked against its abstract or full text. Coverage up to 2026-11-26.

The **In draft** column shows whether the arXiv ID already appears in `paper/main.bib`. BibTeX for every ✗ entry is in [`bib/additional_refs_SBI_MBHB.bib`](bib/additional_refs_SBI_MBHB.bib) (INSPIRE keys).

## 2. MBHB parameter inference on LISA Data Challenge data

> As far as we could find, **no SBI or neural-posterior method has yet been applied to the official Radler LDC1-1 MBHB data**. The SBI papers above use their own simulations; at most they borrow LDC source parameters or priors. Re-check this before stating it in the paper.

> The LDC1-1 reference MCMC posterior (Baker & Marsat) has no accompanying paper that we could find; cite the LDC data release / website for it.
